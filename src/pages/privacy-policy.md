# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** {August 23, 2026}

This page explains what information is collected when you visit or use this website ("the Site"), and how it's handled.

## What we collect

The Site itself does not use analytics, tracking cookies, or any third-party advertising scripts.

The only personal information collected is what you choose to submit through the **Contact** page, which may include:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your message, including the title of any artwork you're inquiring about

## How it's used

Information submitted through the Contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry — for example, to answer questions about available work, pricing, or purchasing.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Third-party processing

Contact form submissions are processed using [Formspree](https://formspree.io), a third-party form-handling service. When you submit the Contact form, your information is transmitted to and stored by Formspree in order to deliver it to us. Formspree's own privacy practices are described at [formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy).

## Data retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, or as retained by Formspree per their own policies.

## Your rights

You may request that any information you've submitted be deleted by contacting us. We will honor such requests within a reasonable timeframe.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, you have rights under the GDPR including access, correction, and erasure of your personal data. If you are a California resident, you have similar rights under the CCPA/CPRA.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to our [Contact page](./contact).
